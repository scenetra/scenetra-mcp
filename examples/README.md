# Example prompts

Paste any of these into Claude, Cursor or another MCP client connected to Scenetra. The agent picks the models, builds the board and runs it.

## Product photo to ad video

> Here's my product photo: <image URL>. Remove the background, place the product on a clean marble surface with soft morning light, then animate it into a 5-second video ad with a slow camera push-in. Use the cheapest good video model.

## One idea, several styles

> Generate the same scene, "a lighthouse on a rocky coast", in four moods: dusk, storm, fog and midday. Share one style prompt across all four, and run them in parallel.

## Character to video

> Create a portrait of a 30-year-old chef in a busy kitchen, then turn it into a 6-second video where she looks up and smiles at the camera. Add native audio.

## Extend an existing board

> On the board you just made, add an upscale step after the video and run only the new step.

## Blueprint only (no credits)

> Design, but don't run, a board that takes one product photo and outputs three ad variations: studio, outdoor and lifestyle. Give me the link so I can review it first.
